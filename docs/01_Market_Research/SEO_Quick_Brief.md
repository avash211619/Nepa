Rationale for keyword choices
- Each keyword maps to a concrete JTBD from our user research (students, bloggers, e-commerce, developers, designers).
- Long-tail queries like "heic to webp bulk" and "generate srcset online" indicate high conversion intent (users looking to accomplish the workflow). These are prioritized for tool pages rather than purely informational articles.
- Keywords cover both single-image privacy-focused flows (remove exif, heic -> jpg) and volume/automation flows (bulk image compression, API-focused queries).

Why these are "easy wins"
- They align with clear JTBD and have high user intent to use a tool (not just read). Tool pages typically rank well when they directly satisfy action intent.
- Competitors often have single-function pages; we can win by combining workflow (convert → compress → responsive output) and providing helpful snippets (HTML, Next.js integration).
- These pages are prime candidates for programmatic generation across formats/targets (e.g., many variations for sizes, marketplaces, frameworks).
