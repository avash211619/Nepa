Growth & SEO Roadmap (6 months)

Phase 1 (0-2 months): Launch core tool pages
- Publish top 20 tool landing pages (from Quick Brief)
- Implement on-page tool UI with schema markup and canonicalization
- Setup GA4, Search Console, PostHog

Phase 2 (2-4 months): Content expansion & programmatic pages
- Expand to ~200 prioritized pages using programmatic URL patterns
- Publish pillar guides (Image Optimization for SEO, WebP vs AVIF)
- Build internal linking per IA

Phase 3 (4-6 months): Conversion optimization
- A/B test CTAs and on-page widgets
- Add related-tools widget to increase session depth
- Begin ad monetization experiments on low-intent pages

KPIs to track
- Optimized images/day (north-star)
- Organic sessions & top-10 keywords
- CTR from landing pages
- Task completion rate

Content & Editorial
